🎳 Gradient Descent — Roll the Ball

The network's goal is to find the lowest point on the loss surface. At every step, it computes the gradient (slope) of the loss and moves the weights downhill by η · gradient. This is gradient descent.

The learning rate η controls step size. Drag the slider below to see what happens: too small and convergence is painfully slow; too large and the ball overshoots and bounces around or diverges entirely.
